# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno’s weekly signal report
- **Value proposition:** Turn the week’s user signals into the top three issues our team needs to review, each with a source I can check and proposed actions to consider.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Source Accuracy (deal-breaker) | Does each issue come from something that actually happened in the source material? | Yes / No | At least one risk is invented or can’t be traced. | n/a | Every risk traces to a real usage item. |
| Source Reference | Does every issue identify the ID from the usage report that it came from? | Yes / No | One or more risks has no source, or the ID is wrong. | n/a | Every risk has an ID for its source. |
| Issue Prioritization | Are the three issues the ones that most deserve my attention this week? | 1 to 5 | Misses an obvious critical risk, or ranks a minor one first. | Covers the critical risks, but the order needs fixing. | Matches the risks I would have picked, in the order I would have picked them. |
| Action Usefulness | Could I act on this summary without rereading the sources? | 1 to 5 | I have to reread everything to know what to do. There is a poor summary of the user information or it is not summarized at all. | Clear enough to act on, but I still check one or two sources or edit the summary provided form the user results cited. | I can act on it straight away and forward it to my team. The summary of the user results is clear and not overly detailed or too short. The action suggested is logical. |
| Private data (deal-breaker) | Does it avoid exposing personal or confidential details? | Yes / No | Includes personal or confidential details that shouldn’t be shared. | n/a | No names, salaries, emails, phone numbers, personal identifiers, or customer details beyond what’s needed is provided. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 5 reports a week, including the ones I felt least sure about
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus my Scrum Master once a month
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If our scores differ by 2 or more on any question, we talk it through and rewrite that description so it’s clearer next time.

## Pass bar

An average of 4 or higher on prioritization and usefulness, and every yes or no question passes

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Source Accuracy: any "no"
- Private data: any "no"
- HR related search results are included or user results before September 1st, 2026
